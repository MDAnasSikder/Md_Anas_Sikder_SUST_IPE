About Me

I'm a B.Sc. Industrial & Production Engineer from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), passionate about applying Artificial Intelligence and Optimization Techniques to revolutionize manufacturing.

My thesis applied Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and Gray Relational Analysis (GRA) on real CNC machining experiments — discovering that tool path sequence matters more than spindle speed or feed rate.

Currently serving as Executive – Resource Operations Management at X Ceramics Group, where I drive production planning, reduce machine downtime, and optimize utility consumption.

Projects & Thesis

Undergraduate Thesis · Feb 2023 – Mar 2024
Tool Path Selection Based Multi-Featured Part Machining and Optimization: An Experimental Approach
Supervised by Ms. Shanta Saha, SUST  ·  Tools: MATLAB · SolidWorks · Fusion 360 · G-code · Minitab · Design Expert
ACO Optimization Applied Ant Colony Optimization to optimize tool path sequencing for a multi-featured aluminum CNC part.
Gray Relational Analysis Used GRA for simultaneous multi-objective optimization of energy, machining time, and MRR.
36 Experimental Runs Conducted Taguchi L36 trials on a HAAS CNC Mini Mill under varied cutting parameters.
Key Finding Tool path sequence is more critical than spindle speed or feed rate across all response parameters.
